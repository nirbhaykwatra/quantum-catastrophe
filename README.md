# Quantum Catastrophe
Quantum Catastrophe is a 2D side-scrolling action platformer which follows a quantum physicist in thee year 2070. An evil corporation has enslaved a group of scientists and you must use your knowledge of quantum physics to find parts and create devices to harness the power of quantum mechanics. Solve puzzles by using superposition, tunneling and entanglement while blasting away bad guys!
